Scenario 1 – empathy mapping

Wellness app VitaFit continuously and passively collects data on users' heart rate, sleep patterns, and physical activity from a smartwatch. It then produces various metrics on app users' wellbeing levels.

This data not only helps app users manage their (general) fitness; it could also offer important health insights and significantly enhance healthcare provision.

The Lowlands - a fictional EU Member State - institutes a policy that promotes integration of such wellness app data into Electronic Health Records (EHRs). The idea behind the policy is to give General Practitioners a more comprehensive view of their patients' lifestyle and possible risk factors and enable earlier intervention.

Scenario 2 - exam practice

Aeolian is a health insurance company in the Lowlands. It wants to develop an LLM-based coach for preventative lifestyle interventions.

The coach would provide personalised advice based on individual's health aims and features. It would be available to all residents in the Lowlands and not only to Aeolian's clients.

Aeolian claims this could importantly reduce healthcare costs and lead to better health outcomes in the country. They say it would also contribute to innovation, as it could lead to discovery of new benchmarks for LLM accuracy.

To train and test its algorithms and make sure they are safe and effective, Aeolian would like to access users' fitness tracking data from the VitaFit app.

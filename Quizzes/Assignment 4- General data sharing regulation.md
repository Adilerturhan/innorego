According to the Directive on Open Data and Re-use of Public Sector Information, all data held by public sector bodies (PSBs) can be requested and accessed by businesses.

*   True
*   **False**

Not all data held by public sector bodies (PSBs) can be requested and accessed by businesses. The Directive excludes certain data from its scope, e.g.:

*   Data for which **third parties hold intellectual property rights**
*   Data **excluded from access** due to reasons of national security, protection of critical infrastructures, or statistical or commercial confidentiality.
*   Data excluded or restricted from access due to **protection of personal data**.
*   Data obtained by PSBs outside of their public task.

**Correct answer**

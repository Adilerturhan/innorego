## 2. Case Study: ShopLink and Article 102 TFEU [6 points]
### Scenario
"ShopLink," a leading online marketplace in the EU, operates both a platform for independent sellers and its own line of "ShopLink Basics" branded products. Recently, ShopLink changed its search algorithm so that its own products appear at the top of search results, regardless of relevance or consumer ratings. Competing sellers claim that this change significantly reduces their visibility and sales.

### Task
Assess whether ShopLink’s conduct may constitute an abuse of a dominant position under **Article 102 TFEU**. In your answer:
- Outline the relevant legal test.
- Apply the test to the facts of the case.
- Consider both possible justifications for ShopLink’s conduct and the enforcement challenges faced by competition authorities.

## Guide to solving problem questions

The following a simple guide that provides you a general idea on the questions must ask yourself when solving problem questions.

When answering a problem question, make sure to:

*   Clearly provide your answer at the beginning as **YES/NO**
*   Cite the relevant **Articles of the Database Directive (DD)** or **Copyright in the Digital Single Market Directive (CDSM)**.
*   Explain the applicable laws and legal criteria then apply those laws and criteria to the facts presented in the Question and explain whether they are fulfilled or not in that specific instance.
*   Make sure you cite applicable **cases**.

---

### I. Can the database be protected under copyright or sui generis database right?

**1. Is it a database within definition of Database Directive?**

> **Definition [Art 1(2) DD]:** A collection of independent works, data or other materials arranged in a systematic or methodical way and individually accessible by electronic or other means.

**2. If answer to Q. 1 is YES, can database obtain protection under copyright?**

> **Criteria for protection [Article 3(1) DD]:** Does selection/arrangement of database must fulfill AOIC standard of originality by qualifying as its author's own intellectual creation?

**3. If answer to Q. 1 is YES, can database obtain protection under *sui generis* right?**

> **Preliminary criteria to benefit from *sui generis* right [Article 11].**
>
> **Criteria for protection [Article 7]:** Has there been a substantial investment made in either the obtaining, verification or presentation of contents of database.

---

### II. Has there been infringement of copyright or *sui generis* database right or breach of contract?

**4. If the answers to either Q. 2 and/or Q.3 are YES, then has the user engaged in acts coming within the scope of protection without the authorization of the database rightsholder?**

> If answer to Q. 2 is yes, do the acts the user engaged in come within scope of **exclusive copyright** [Article 5]?
>
> If answer to Q. 3 is yes, do the acts the user engaged in come within scope of **exclusive *sui generis* right** [Article 7]?

**5. If the answers to both Q. 2 and Q.3 are NO, are there any contractual provisions that regulate the way in which the database can be used? Has the user violated any of these terms and conditions?**

---

### III. Can user benefit from exceptions for data-mining?

**6. If the answers to either Q. 2 and/or Q.3 are YES, then:**

> **Can user benefit from exception for scientific research under Art.3?**
>
> *   Does user qualify as a beneficiary of the exception [i.e. lawful access to database through a research organization and cultural heritage institution that fulfills criteria to benefit from Art. 3 exception]?
> *   Has the data-mining been engaged in for a scientific purpose?
> *   Has data been mined and stored in accordance with conditions set out in the exception?
> *   Are there any circumstances that prevent user from benefitting from exception?

**7. Can user benefit from general exception under Art. 4?**

> *   Has the database rightsholder opted-out of exception? If yes, has this opt-out been exercised in accordance with the conditions set out in Recital 18?
> *   If answer to above is no, does user qualify as a beneficiary of the exception [i.e. does user have lawful access to database]?
> *   Has data been mined and stored in accordance with conditions set out in the exception?

## Legislation & Case Law
- **US Communications Decency Act (CDA), Section 230:** Provides immunity for online platforms from liability for third-party content.

# Module 2: Health Data Sharing

## EU Legislation
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation (EU) 2016/679):**
    - **Art. 4:** Definitions (e.g., 'personal data', 'processing', 'data concerning health').
    - **Art. 5:** Principles relating to processing of personal data (lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, etc.).
    - **Art. 6:** Lawfulness of processing (legal bases).
    - **Art. 9:** Processing of special categories of personal data (e.g., health data).
    - **Chapter 3:** Rights of the data subject (access, rectification, erasure, etc.).
    - **Chapter 4:** Obligations of controllers and processors.
    - **Art. 89(1):** Safeguards and derogations relating to processing for scientific research purposes.

- **Data Governance Act (DGA) (Regulation (EU) 2022/868):**
    - **Chapter 2 (Arts. 3-5):** Reuse of certain categories of data held by public sector bodies.
    - **Chapter 3:** Requirements for data intermediation services.
    - **Chapter 4:** Data altruism.

- **Data Act (DA) (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854):**
    - **Arts. 3–5:** Rules for B2C and B2B data sharing for IoT products.
    - **Arts. 8–9:** Rules on fairness and compensation for data access.
    - **Chapter 5:** B2G data sharing based on exceptional need.

- **European Health Data Space (EHDS) Regulation:**
    - **Art. 1:** Establishes the EHDS.
    - **Art. 2(2)(d) & Ch. II:** Defines and regulates the **primary use** of health data (for healthcare).
        - **MyHealth@EU:** Cross-border infrastructure for primary use.
    - **Art. 2(2)(e) & Ch. IV:** Defines and regulates the **secondary use** of health data (for research, innovation, etc.).
        - **HealthData@EU:** Infrastructure for secondary use.
    - **Arts. 53-54:** Delineation of allowed and prohibited purposes for secondary use.

# Module 3: Competition Law

## EU Legislation & Case Law
- **Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU):**
    - **Art. 101 TFEU:** Prohibits anti-competitive agreements (cartels).
    - **Art. 102 TFEU:** Prohibits the abuse of a dominant market position.
- **EU Merger Regulation (EUMR) (Regulation (EC) No 139/2004):** Controls concentrations (mergers and acquisitions) with an EU dimension.
- **Case COMP/C-3/37.792, Microsoft (2004):** Found Microsoft abused its dominant position by withholding interoperability information.
- **Case C-252/21, Meta Platforms (formerly Facebook) (2023):** German case where the CJEU clarified that data protection (GDPR) violations can be considered in an abuse of dominance assessment.

## US Legislation
- **Sherman Antitrust Act (1890):**
    - **Section 1:** Prohibits contracts, combinations, or conspiracies in restraint of trade.
    - **Section 2:** Prohibits monopolization, attempts to monopolize, or conspiracies to monopolize.

# Module 4: IP & Database Protection

## EU Legislation & Case Law
- **Database Directive (DD) (Directive 96/9/EC):**
    - **Art. 3:** Provides copyright protection for original databases (Author's Own Intellectual Creation - AOIC).
    - **Art. 7:** Provides a *sui generis* right for non-original databases that required a substantial investment.
    - **Art. 11:** Defines who can benefit from the *sui generis* right.
- **Copyright in the Digital Single Market (DSM) Directive (Directive (EU) 2019/790):**
    - **Art. 3:** Mandatory exception for text and data mining (TDM) for scientific research.
    - **Art. 4:** Mandatory exception for TDM for any purpose, with an opt-out mechanism for rightholders.
- **Case C-203/02, BHB v William Hill (2004):** Clarified that investment in the *creation* of data does not count towards the "substantial investment" for the *sui generis* right.
- **Case C-30/14, Ryanair Ltd v PR Aviation BV (2015):** Ruled that contractual restrictions on data use are enforceable for databases not protected by the Database Directive.

# Module 5: AI & Data Regulation

## EU Legislation & Guidelines
- **AI Act (EU Proposal):** A risk-based framework for regulating AI systems. Classifies AI into unacceptable, high, and low/minimal risk categories.
    - **Art. 10:** Allows processing of special category data to ensure bias monitoring, detection, and correction for high-risk AI systems.
- **AI High-Level Expert Group (AI HLEG):**
    - *Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI:* Outlines 7 key requirements for trustworthy AI (human agency, robustness, privacy, transparency, fairness, well-being, and accountability).

## US Legislation & Case Law
- **Van Buren v. United States, 593 U.S. ___ (2021):** Supreme Court decision clarifying the scope of "exceeds authorized access" under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA).
- **LinkedIn Corp. v. hiQ Labs Inc.:** A long-running legal battle over data scraping from public profiles, addressing issues under the CFAA and contract law.
- **Ryanair DAC v. Booking Holdings Inc.:** US case confirming that violating terms of use for a public website without an authentication mechanism does not trigger CFAA liability.
